What TherAssist Does

A clinical assistant built around review, not automation.

AI Scribe Session Capture

Paste a meeting link and the AI Scribe joins and transcribes for you — or capture via pasted transcript, typed notes, or a photo of handwritten notes. Nothing is analyzed automatically, and no audio or video is ever stored.

AI Session Analysis, Reviewed by You

A structured draft — summary, themes, interventions, risk flags, and a suggested focus for next time — generated per session, always reviewed and approved by you before it's saved.

Client-Facing Summaries

A separate, client-safe summary generated from your approved analysis, editable before sharing, with copy, share, email, export, and translate controls.

Case Intelligence

An AI-generated rollup across a client's approved sessions — progress, recurring themes, triggers, protective factors, and breakthroughs — regenerated as new sessions are added.

Treatment Plans & Clinical Tools

Formulations, assessments, a Next Session Planner, a Homework Ideas bank, and a Support Network tab — structured clinical tooling alongside the AI.

Find Similar Cases

Semantic search across your caseload from within a client's profile, surfacing comparable cases in seconds.

Confidentiality, Privacy & Security

Private by architecture, not by policy.

Client identity and clinical content are encrypted on your device before anything leaves it, using a key derived from a passphrase only you know. TherAssist's servers — including the person operating this deployment — never have access to that key and cannot read client data, in plaintext or otherwise, at any point. AI processing only happens for clients you've explicitly marked as consenting to it, and only the transcript text you paste in is ever sent anywhere.

Multi-therapist deployments are fully isolated — an administrator can manage accounts and quotas, but can't read your clients' data, session content, or AI output, and never learns your passphrase.
