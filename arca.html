The Name

Look twice.

ARCA connects data, people, and technology into one focused platform for behavioral professionals. It's built around the same thinking that drives ABA itself: observe behavior, understand it, apply meaningful intervention, measure progress, and use that measurement to guide what comes next. ARCA brings that same discipline into software — a structured environment for therapists to collect data, follow intervention, and see progress, without the technology getting in the way of the clinical work itself.

What ARCA Does

Everything a session needs, structured the way ABA already works.

Session Mode

Live trial-by-trial data collection with full and verbal prompt hierarchies, probe and teaching trial modes, per-target latency timing, and real-time IOA reliability checks between therapists.

Programs & Targets

Mastery criteria, prompt-fading ladders, task analysis and chaining, and program templates your clinic can reuse across every child.

Behavior Tracking

ABC data, frequency, duration, and interval recording, plus a built-in token economy for in-session reinforcement.

Assessments

Standardized assessment tracking, FBA and FA condition logging, and structured preference assessments.

On-Device Reporting

Session, progress, behavior, and treatment-summary PDF reports generated entirely on-device — nothing rendered on a server.

Multi-Therapist Clinics

Role-based access for therapists, supervisors, and admins, mid-session handover, and local-network sync between paired clinic devices.

See It In Action

Real screens. Real workflow.

Everything a session needs, on one screen.

Prompt levels, behavior tracking, IOA checks, and a live session summary — all visible at once, so nothing pulls attention away from the child in front of you.

A clear read on your caseload.

Active children, today's sessions, progress trends, and behavior summaries — the moment you log in.

Reports that write themselves.

Session reports, progress reports, treatment summaries, and parent-friendly reports — generated on-device in a click, ready to print or share.

Privacy & Confidentiality

Private by architecture, not by policy.

Every child's profile, every trial, every note — stored only on the device that recorded it. Technothera never has access to it, at any point, for any reason.
