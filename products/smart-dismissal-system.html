The Workflow

Eight steps. End to end.

1
Parent Opens App

No download needed — works in any smartphone browser

2
Taps Pickup Request

~10 minutes before arriving at the nursery

3
Display Board Updates

Child's name appears on the TV display instantly

4
Teacher Notified

Silent push notification sent directly to teacher's phone

5
Teacher Marks Ready

6
Parent Taps Arrived

7

The System

Four interfaces. One system.

Interface 1 of 4

Live Display Screen

Installed on a large TV or monitor in the staff area — always on, always updating. The command center of the entire dismissal process.

  • Child full name, large and bold for instant visibility
  • Class name with unique colour-coded dot per class
  • Live countdown timer per pickup request
  • ARRIVED badge — pulses urgently when parent is at door
  • READY shown in green when child is prepared
  • Auto-sorted: Arrived → Ready → Countdown

Safety First
