At a Glance

What ARCA does

Live session mode

Trial-by-trial data collection with full and verbal prompt hierarchies, probe and teaching trial modes, and real-time IOA reliability checks between therapists.

Programs, targets & behavior tracking

Mastery criteria, prompt-fading ladders, ABC and frequency/duration data, plus a built-in token economy for in-session reinforcement.

On-device reporting

Session, progress, behavior, and treatment-summary PDF reports generated entirely on-device — nothing rendered on a server.

Fully offline, multi-therapist

Works with no internet connection, with role-based access and local-network sync between paired clinic devices.
