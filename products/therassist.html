The Core Differentiator

Privacy that’s provable, not promised.

TherAssist’s privacy model has two tiers — and being precise about what it can mathematically guarantee versus what it minimizes as carefully as possible is itself part of the story.

Tier 1 · Everything You Type
True Zero-Knowledge

Covers client identity, all clinical notes, session analyses, formulations, treatment plans, and intake data — everything the therapist types, pastes, or already has on their device.

  • Encryption key derived entirely on-device from a passphrase the therapist chooses — never transmitted anywhere, ever
  • PBKDF2 at 600,000 iterations, AES-256-GCM
  • The paying account’s activation code is fully decoupled from the passphrase
  • Every synced document is encrypted client-side before it ever leaves the device — the sync relay only ever stores and moves ciphertext
  • Not even Technothera can read a therapist’s client data — no admin override, no support-team backdoor
Tier 2 · Meeting-Bot Transcription
Hybrid Encryption

For the one channel that structurally has to touch a server: a meeting bot has to run somewhere other than the therapist’s own browser.

  • Every device generates its own RSA-OAEP keypair on first unlock; the private key never leaves the device
  • A transcript is hybrid-encrypted to that specific therapist’s public key before it’s ever written to any database
  • Verified directly: decrypts correctly with the right key, fails entirely with any other therapist’s key
  • The encrypted result sits in a short-lived holding area, deleted from the server the moment the therapist’s device picks it up
  • The meeting-bot vendor only ever receives an opaque job reference and a meeting link — never a client’s name or case history

Clinical depth, not just transcription

Case Intelligence

An auto-generated, regenerable rollup across a client’s recent sessions: progress, recurring themes, protective factors.

Next Session Planner

Discussion points, follow-up questions, and homework ideas drawn from the last five approved sessions plus the active treatment plan.

Clinical Formulations

CBT, DBT, and ACT as independent, coexisting entries per client, with an “Integrate Formulations” synthesis view.

Treatment Plans & Hypothesis Log

Formal treatment plans plus a running scratchpad for working theories, confirmed or revised over time.

Assessment Library

PHQ-9, GAD-7, BAI and more, with automatic score trends — or upload a completed questionnaire and let AI extract it.

Intake

Type manually or upload a document; AI extracts clinical fields and identity details to pre-fill the client’s profile.

Outcome Tracking

Pre- and post-scores as real structured data, making “cases where ACT worked well” an actual answerable question.

Case Closure & Archive

Closed cases move to a fully searchable Archive, restorable anytime, with retention-aware safeguards before deletion.

Bilingual By Default

Built for how bilingual therapists actually think.

Every session is treated as Egyptian Arabic + English with natural code-switching, reasoned about and generated that way natively — not translated after the fact.

  • Generated bilingually in a single AI call — pick which language to view, copy, or export at the moment of sharing
  • A global setting for AI output: English-only, Arabic-only, or the natural Arabic/English clinical mix
  • Native RTL/LTR rendering throughout, with automatic text-direction detection
  • PDF exports render Arabic and English correctly via the browser’s own text-shaping engine

Scope Note

TherAssist is a focused clinical documentation and intelligence tool — not an EHR or clinic-management platform, and not a tool that makes clinical decisions: it drafts, and the therapist reviews and approves everything before it becomes part of the record. And it’s not a tool where “we promise not to look” is the whole privacy story — where it matters most, the architecture makes it structurally impossible for anyone but the treating therapist to read the data.
