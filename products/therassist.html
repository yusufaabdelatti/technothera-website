TherAssist

The only person who can read your notes is you.

TherAssist is a bilingual (Arabic/English), local-first clinical intelligence system for psychotherapists — it turns session transcripts, handwritten notes, or a live meeting recording bot into structured, reviewable clinical records, and turns years of practice into a searchable, growing body of clinical knowledge, while making it structurally impossible for anyone but the treating therapist — not even Technothera itself — to ever read a client’s actual clinical content.

What TherAssist does

Four ways to capture a session

Live meeting bot, transcript paste, typed notes, or a photo of handwritten notes — AI drafts a structured clinical note, you review before anything is saved.

Zero-knowledge encryption

The therapist’s own passphrase is the only key — never transmitted, never known to Technothera. Structurally impossible for anyone else to read.

A growing clinical knowledge base

Full-text and semantic search turn years of sessions into a searchable, filterable personal record — not just scattered notes.

Bilingual from the start

Generated natively in Arabic and English — not translated after the fact.
