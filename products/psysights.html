PsySights

Psychological assessment, digitized. From paper questionnaires to clinic-branded clinical reports — in minutes.

PsySights is a psychological insights platform built for psychiatric and psychological clinics worldwide. It transforms manual, paper-based assessment workflows into a unified digital platform — without changing the validated instruments clinicians already trust.

The Workflow

Ten steps. Assessment to report.

1
Assessment Selection

Clinician browses the catalog, filtered by population, rater, and language

2
Pre-Screen

Overview of what the instrument measures, who completes it, and estimated time

3
Delivery Choice

Complete in-platform now, or send a secure remote link to the patient

4
Taking the Assessment

One item at a time, auto-saved, RTL/LTR follows the selected language

5
Completion

Patient sees only a thank-you screen — no scores, no interpretation

6
Automatic Scoring

Raw scores, subscales, T-scores, and classification bands computed instantly

7
AI-Assisted Narrative

A structured clinical write-up drafted for clinician review and editing

8
Report Generation

Polished PDF with clinic branding, results, charts, and narrative

9
Delivery & Storage

Download, print, or email — securely stored, scoped to that clinic only

10
Longitudinal Tracking

Re-administer over time and compare results across sessions visually

Assessment Catalog

A growing catalog of validated instruments

Every assessment is represented as a structured configuration — items, subscales, scoring, and norms — so new instruments can be added without changing the platform.

Adult Personality & Psychopathology

MMPI-2, including full validity and clinical scales with T-score computation

Mood & Anxiety Screening

Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II), plus anxiety screening in the style of BAI/PSWQ

Personality Profiling

Big Five Personality Test across the OCEAN dimensions

Child Behavioral Screening (Parent-Report)

Child Behavior Checklist (CBCL/6-18), Conners’ Parent Rating Scale (CPRS-R:L)

Autism Spectrum Assessment

CARS-2, clinician-administered and parent/caregiver checklist forms

Adaptive Behavior Assessment

Vineland Adaptive Behavior Scales, 3rd Edition — semi-structured clinician interview

Design Principles

What makes PsySights different

The instrument is sacred

Validated scoring logic is never altered. Only the delivery changes.

Bilingual by design

Full RTL/LTR layout switching, not just mirrored text.

Every clinic is its own walled garden

Row-level data isolation. No cross-clinic visibility, ever.

The clinician stays in control

Patients never see their own scores or interpretations.

Your brand, not ours

Every report carries the clinic’s logo and clinician’s name. PsySights appears only as a small footer mark.
